1 : Very dry - no soil moisture measurable in the profile; soils dry throughout the growing season
2 : Dry - soil moisture limited; moisture only in the upper profile and depleted by mid-season

4 : Moderately moist - moisture available in the upper profile through most of the growing season
5 : Moist - moisture present in the upper profile through the growing season with few deficits
6 : Wet - moisture in the upper profile through most of the growing season; occasional brief deficits
